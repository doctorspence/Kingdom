## The project in one paragraph
KINGDOM (working title) is a dark fantasy co-op action RPG in Godot 4.7.2 (GDScript), host-and-play for 1-10 players, Souls-style combat plus a WoW-style hotbar. The owner has no coding experience.

**Combat rhythm pass (2026-11-22, now in `main`):** Sekiro / Nioh 2-style timing. Deflect = a timed guard per blow (mashing shrinks the window); enemies have a visible balance bar that hits and deflects drain, and an empty bar opens a riposte; enemies guard after 2 hits in a row and strike back, string attacks together and sometimes delay a swing; "Steady" (tap guard as your swing recovers) refunds stamina; perfect dodges arm a stronger counter, and rolling into a red attack (Step-in) rocks the attacker. Pass 3 added warning sounds (glint "shing", red-attack rattle), rising clangs for deflects in a row, "Flawless" strings, a heavy crack when balance breaks, and "Interrupted" (a hit during your light attack's wind-up knocks it away). Pass 4 added attack reach (swings step in up to 1.2 m to land) and enemies that punish a held guard with their red or heaviest attack. Then (owner's request) heavies got a slower wind-up and jump attacks a downward chop and slam (clips cut in `character_model.gd`). Rules in `DESIGN.md` 5.4-5.5.1, numbers in `data/balance/player_tuning.tres` and the enemy files, tests in `tests/combat_check.gd`. Waiting for the owner's playtest (`TODO.md`).

**Movement redesign, hybrid (2026-11-23, branch `greenwood`):** grounded "weight" from the owner's brief (`DESIGN_INBOX.md`, Filed). Exploring stays camera-relative; **combat stance** (locked on, or guard up) faces the foe, or where the camera looks if no target, and slows the backpedal (0.7×); **moving sideways is a slow side-step (1.3 m/s, owner asked "side step slowly like Gloria Victis")**: the player holds the fighting-stance pose (`idle_anim`) and `game/characters/stance_steps.gd` (StanceSteps, a SkeletonModifier3D with two-bone leg IK) steps the feet out and in with planted feet fixed to the ground (movement_check measures drift < 3 cm, lift ~9 cm). It runs for any human model moving in a standing pose, so guard-walking and enemies step too. Walk 1.5, run 5.8, sprint 8.0; acceleration 16, deceleration 20, turning a third of before (all in `player_tuning.gd` / `data/balance/player_tuning.tres`). The backpedal plays the walk or jog in reverse (`CharacterModel.update_locomotion(..., backward)`); sidesteps turn the hips and legs toward the step while the spine keeps the chest on the foe (`game/characters/leg_twist.gd`, a SkeletonModifier3D added by `CharacterModel`, so enemies and other players get it too). Stride numbers were measured from the animations (`tests/stride_check.gd`: walk 0.95, jog 5.25, sprint 7.6 m/s) and the legs no longer over-spin, so planted feet stop skating. Pictures: `tests/visual_stance.gd`. **Mouse wheel zooms the camera** (1.6-6 m, `player_camera.gd`) when not locked on, and still switches target when locked. `movement_check` covers stance speeds; combat, moves and zone checks pass. Not playtested by hand yet.

**Base Outcast outfit, code stand-in (2026-11-23, branch `greenwood`):** every player now wears the base Outcast clothes from `DESIGN.md` 12.1 under empty gear slots: linen shirt (laced neckline, rolled sleeves), wool trousers, wrapped boots, belt with pouch, satchel strap and faded red cloth, olive cloak with the hood down and a bronze clasp, hair, plus a beard (broad body) or a braid (slender body, lower neckline). The faces stay the blank mannequin. A real hand-painted model needs the Tripo GLB, which needs a paid Tripo plan to export. Not playtested by hand yet.
